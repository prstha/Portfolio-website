The short version

My path wasn't a straight line. I started in electronics engineering, moved to business school, and ended up in the one job that needs a bit of both.

Enough tech to follow the engineers. Enough business to keep the client calm. Since my first sprint in 2019, I've been the person in the middle making sure everyone agrees on what we're building, when it's due, and what might get in the way.

Lately that means AI and cloud projects, a lot of Jira, and status reports people actually read.
