01 / About

Delivery that people can plan around.

I'm a results-driven Senior Project Manager based in Nepal with 8+ years in the IT industry and 4+ years leading project and delivery management. I drive end-to-end execution of web, mobile and cloud solutions using Agile practices.

Today my focus is on AI and cloud programs: GenAI, LLM-based and data-driven solutions for clients in energy and healthcare, with a strong emphasis on quality and scalability.

What I care about most is predictable delivery: the kind where stakeholders trust the dates, risks surface early, and teams have the space to do their best work.

  • Predictable delivery

    Sprint cadence, milestone planning and capacity management that keep commitments realistic.

  • Risk & dependencies

    Dashboards that make blockers, risks and dependencies visible before they become surprises.

  • High-performing teams

    Cross-functional coordination across engineering, QA, design, data and product, plus mentoring the next PMs.

  • Stakeholder clarity

    Weekly reports, demos and joint planning that give clients and leadership a clear view of progress.
